Our colleagues, with decades of experience, support clients in legal, tax, audit, and accounting matters. The focus is simple: clear solutions, realistic timelines, and audit-ready execution.

Partner

Dr. Zoltán Pályi

Managing Partner

An economist with a PhD and an MBA, he is also a certified public accountant, tax expert, and holds qualifications in International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) and International Treasury Management (CertITM). His professional career began at the Ministry of Finance's VAT and Customs Department, where he spent four years working on legislation and the EU harmonisation of taxation. Subsequently, he gained extensive leadership and professional experience as a finance manager at several major multinational companies, including the finance departments of PMI and T-Mobile Hungary. Between 2006 and 2013, he led the Tax and Treasury team at Tesco-Global Zrt., and in 2012 he also took on financial controller responsibilities. In 2014, he joined a Hungarian consulting firm as a managing partner, where he managed the accounting, payroll and bookkeeping business, and in 2015 he joined Ecovis Hungary as a partner. He has extensive experience in finance, accounting and taxation, particularly in the FMCG, telecommunications and banking sectors. Since 2011, he has been an honorary associate professor at Szent István University, where he regularly gives lectures, supervises theses and serves as a member of the state examination board. He is also an active lecturer at the Faculty of Law of Eötvös Loránd University, contributing to the training of the next generation of professionals.

Katalin Bercsényi

Tax Advisor Manager

Katalin Bercsényi graduated from Corvinus University of Budapest with a Bachelor's degree in Finance and Accounting and a Master's degree in Accounting, and is also a certified public accountant and registered tax advisor. She has been a member of the Ecovis team since April 2017, starting as an intern, then as a junior and senior consultant, and since March 2026 as a manager. She provides tax and accounting advice to domestic and international clients. She has extensive experience in tax due diligence, corporate restructuring and tax audit support. She is also involved in the preparation and review of tax returns, support for closing processes, preparation of transfer pricing documentation, as well as drafting requests for rulings and other tax authority communications and appeals. She supports her clients in both Hungarian and English.
